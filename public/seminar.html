Pharmacovigilance & FAERS signal detection

A live walkthrough of disproportionality analysis—and where artificial intelligence fits in the safety surveillance lifecycle.

What is pharmacovigilance?

Science and activities for detecting, assessing, and preventing adverse drug reactions (ADRs) after marketing authorization.

What is FAERS?

The FDA Adverse Event Reporting System—spontaneous reports from clinicians, patients, and manufacturers. DEMO, DRUG, and REAC tables joined on primaryid.

Why manual signal detection is hard

  • Millions of drug–event pairs; rare events hide in noise.
  • Reporting bias, duplicate cases, and confounding by indication.
  • Analysts need repeatable statistics and clinical judgment—not slide decks alone.

Disproportionality analysis

For each drug–ADR pair we build a 2×2 contingency table across all cases in the dataset:

Event of interestAll other events
Drug of interestab
All other drugscd

PRR = (a/(a+b)) ÷ (c/(c+d))
ROR = (a×d) / (b×c)

χ² (Yates-corrected) tests association. This app flags a signal when PRR ≥ 2, χ² ≥ 4, and cases ≥ 3—the FDA/MHRA-style screen used in faers_signal_detection.py.

Where AI & ML fit

NLP on narratives

Free-text case descriptions can be mined for symptoms, negation, and temporal cues—complementing structured MedDRA coding.

ML prioritisation

Models can rank drug–event pairs for human review using features beyond PRR (plausibility, novelty, seriousness).

Bayesian extensions

Regulatory systems often use BCPNN or EBGM—Bayesian shrinkage toward background rates, not just frequentist screens.

Limits & regulation

Signals ≠ causality. AI outputs need validation, audit trails, and alignment with ICH E2B/E2E and FDA pharmacovigilance guidance.

Summary

  • FAERS provides the real-world safety data backbone; disproportionality (PRR, ROR, χ²) scales screening across thousands of pairs.
  • This app implements the same formulas as the Python pipeline—with upload, drug mapping, and comparison views for hands-on exploration.
  • AI/ML augments—not replaces—expert review: NLP for narratives, learning-to-rank for triage, Bayesian methods in production vigilance systems.
  • Always pair statistical signals with clinical context and regulatory process.
